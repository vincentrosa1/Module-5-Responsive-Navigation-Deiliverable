Questions about your plot, an accessible visit, or getting involved? Start here.

Plot help

Irrigation leak, pest problem, or need to give up your bed mid-season? Let us know and a garden coordinator will follow up within two days.

Accessibility

Main paths are packed decomposed granite and wide enough for wheelchairs. Tell us ahead of time if you need a raised bed, seating, or help carrying tools.

Contact us

Email hello@rivercitygarden.example or call (916) 555-0123.

Volunteer

Help with Saturday workdays, harvest donation runs to food pantries, or the children's planting table. No experience needed, and gloves are provided.
