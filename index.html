Grow something in the River City

River City Community Garden is a neighborhood nonprofit in Sacramento. Members grow their own food, learn from each other, and share extra harvest with local food pantries.

Garden plots

We rent 4 X 8 ft raised beds for $40 a year, water included. Two wheelchair height beds are held for gardeners who need them.

Plots open each February, just ahead of the spring planting season. Join the waitlist any time at the front shed or by contacting us.

Workshops

  • Watering Through a Valley Summer: first Saturday, 8 a.m.
  • Tomatoes 101: second Saturday in March, 9 a.m.
  • Composting Basics: third Sunday, 10 a.m.

Workshops are free and open to everyone, members or not.
