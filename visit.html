Plan your visit

Everything you need before you come out: when the gates open, where to find us, and how to get here.

Hours

  • May–Sptember: 6 a.m. to 11 a.m. and 5 p.m. to sunset
  • October–April: 8 a.m. to sunset
  • Open every day of the year

Summer hours skip the afternoon so nobody is working the beds in triple-digit heat.

Location

1850 River City Way, Sacramento. The entrance gate faces the bike path.

Getting here

Free street parking on both sides of the block. Bike racks sit right inside the gate, and the nearest bus stop is a two-minute walk.
